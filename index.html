My story

Why I do this

I've spent 15+ years around energy — as a founder, a CTO, and a licensed electrician who still gets his hands dirty. I've built power and storage systems that run in the field around the world, and I run my own electrical and automation company today.

Now I'm doing a master's in chemistry at Aalto University in Finland. I'm there because I wanted to understand energy storage so deeply that I can one day have a role in developing an organic energy storage in big scale.

Here's what bugs me: this stuff is drowning in hype and sales talk, and people are making big decisions half-blind. So I decided to just explain it honestly — the science, the wiring, and the real math on whether it pays off. That's the whole idea behind Battery Chemist.
